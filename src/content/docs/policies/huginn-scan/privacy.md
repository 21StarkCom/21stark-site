_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

## 1. What Huginn Scan is

Sleipnir Huginn Scan ("Scan") is a Chrome extension that runs an accessibility scan of the page you are viewing, when
you start one from its toolbar button, and shows the issues it finds. The scanning engine is packaged inside the extension. It is an
internal tool, distributed privately.

## 2. What Scan reads

Only the page you scan, and only when you start a scan: its content and structure, which the packaged engine
checks for accessibility issues. Scan uses Chrome's `activeTab` permission, so it can reach a page only after you
click it there.

## 3. Where information goes

Nowhere. Scan has no native host and **sends nothing to any server**, including ours. It loads only files from its
own package.

## 4. What Scan stores

Scan results are kept in Chrome's session storage for the extension, on your computer, and are cleared when the
browser closes or the extension reloads. Scan keeps nothing else.

## 5. What we never do

We do not collect, sell, rent or share any information from Scan, use it for advertising or lending decisions, or
use it to train AI models.
